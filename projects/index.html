VANTAGE NOS3 Digital Twin

Operational Digital Twin of STP-H12-VANTAGE Payload developed through NSF-SHREC & NASA IV&V

Graduate Research Project creating VANTAGE Digital Twin on top of NOS3 simulator


Project Summary:

Designed, Implemented, & Iterated VANTAGE Digital Twin in NOS3 with Orbit-Accurate Camera Capture of RGB & Event-based Images

  • Created 2 high fidelity camera simulators interfaced with cFS, COSMOS ground software, and simulated hardware protocols all in NOS3
  • Utilized NASA GIBS API for GPS-linked LEO satellite imagery
  • Utilized Video 2 Events (V2E) to create inertially-accurate neuromorphic frames with VANTAGE-specific parameters
  • Piped GPS data into camera sims via cFS at 1Hz to inform satellite imagery coordinates and velocities for accurate image retrival
  • Decoupled & synced camera captures for the 2 simulators with flight accurate, high quality, internal database, and sample captures.
  • Created custom docker images for camera simulator functionality



  • Developed high fidelity simulation system architecture for VANTAGE digital twin
  • Designed modular component simulator layers to achieve optimal testing fidelity and depth via QEMU, NOS3, and real hardware
